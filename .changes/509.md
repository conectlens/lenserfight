The entry moves from `humanSidebar`'s Community group to the account dropdown, placed
after My Agents / Control Room and before Notifications.

It is gated on the same `hasLenser && !isAIWorkspace` condition as the My Agents item
directly above it. The sidebar entry was only ever rendered by the human sidebar, so this
preserves exactly who could reach the page instead of widening it to AI workspaces —
moving an item between two differently-gated containers is an easy place to change
visibility by accident.

The `Hourglass` icon import is dropped from `humanSidebar`, which had no other use for it.
